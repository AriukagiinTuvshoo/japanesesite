# N2 resource research and curation

Reviewed on 2026-10-07. This is a curated starting directory, not a claim to have found every N2 resource online. Links, access rules, pricing, and content can change; check each provider before relying on it.

- **File:** [`756883766-KORE-DE-GOUKAKU-N2-part-1.pdf`](https://github.com/AriukagiinTuvshoo/japanesesite/blob/main/756883766-KORE-DE-GOUKAKU-N2-part-1.pdf)

- **Scope stated by the book:** approximately 3,500 vocabulary entries and 426 vocabulary questions across six item types; 135 grammar items grouped into 15 chapters; reading and listening practice; two mock tests with answer material and listening scripts, with audio referenced by QR code.

- The PDF is linked on GitHub `main`; it is not copied into the app repository branch or the PWA offline cache. An explicit redistribution license was not located in the PDF text/metadata inspected, so rights to mirror or repackage the file remain unverified.

## App-created N2 practice expansion

The in-app N2 set is original study content, not a copy of the linked book or official JLPT sample questions. Current app totals are **156 N2 vocabulary entries**, **76 N2 grammar lessons**, **24 N2 reading items**, and **23 N2 listening items**. The recent expansion added 80 vocabulary entries, 40 grammar lessons, 15 reading items, and 14 listening prompts; older short notices and passages were also extended or reclassified where needed for the mock.

The full-timing simulation contains **85 original questions** by app design:

| Practice section | App question allocation | Formats represented |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Vocabulary | 30 (5 each) | Kanji reading, orthography, word formation, contextual meaning, paraphrase, and usage |
| Grammar | 15 (5 each) | Form selection, sentence composition, and text grammar |
| Reading | 20 (4 each) | Short, mid-size, integrated, thematic, and information retrieval |
| Listening | 20 (4 each) | Task-based comprehension, key points, general outline, quick response, and integrated comprehension |

The simulation uses **105 minutes for Language Knowledge + Reading**, followed by a break screen and **50 minutes for Listening**, matching the N2 time allocation shown in the official timing reference above. The 85-question distribution is an app-authored sampling choice: JLPT materials describe question purposes and formats but do not publish a fixed per-format count for this mock to copy. The app explicitly describes the mock as an approximation—not official material and not an exact official item-count replica. Practice scores remain raw learning metrics, not JLPT scaled scores or pass predictions.

Reading passages are authored around the official format descriptions and approximate length guidance: roughly 200 characters for short, 500 for mid-size, 600 total for integrated, 900 for thematic long, and 700 for information-retrieval texts. Lengths and difficulty are design targets, not certified equivalences to an official test. Listening dialogue is original Japanese text played with device/browser text-to-speech, not official or studio audio. Every generated item has localized Mongolian, English, and Japanese prompts, answer options, and explanations; reading passages also include kana support and translations.

## Coverage and copyright decisions

- The resource shelf groups sources as **official**, **independent reference**, or **supplemental practice**. Descriptions state when a resource is easier than N2, may require an account, or publishes non-official estimates.
- The official JLPT site describes level abilities, test structure, item purposes, timing, and scoring. It does not provide a definitive N2 vocabulary/grammar checklist or a fixed per-format question count for this app’s mock. Third-party vocabulary/kanji/grammar indexes differ and are not advertised in this project as a complete official syllabus.
- The site writes original exercises and links to official or third-party sources rather than scraping or republishing their protected question banks. The 85-question N2 simulation follows broad format and timing references but uses an app-designed distribution, so it is only an approximation.
- Book progress is stored locally in the browser, separately from the existing quiz scheduler and learning progress.
